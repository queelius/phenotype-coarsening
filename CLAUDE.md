## Project Overview

Academic paper repository: **Electronic phenotyping as coarsening at
random: identifiability of clinical states from diagnosis codes.**

Conference-format target (~12 pages). Fourth paper in a series applying
the masked-data identifiability framework to new application domains,
after `papers/scrna-coarsening/` (scRNA-seq) and
`papers/spatial-coarsening/` (spatial transcriptomics).

## Build Commands

```bash
make paper      # builds main.pdf
make sim        # runs scripts/run.R, writes results.rds
make figures    # writes figures/*.pdf from results.rds
make clean      # removes artifacts
```

## Architecture

- `main.tex`: pure-LaTeX top-level with preamble + `\input{sections/...}`
- `sections/`: 8 section files (no `\end{document}` in section files)
- `refs.bib`: BibTeX
- `figures/`: PDF figures (generated by `scripts/figures.R`)
- `scripts/`: simulation/analysis scripts, base R only
  - `sim.R`: DGP, estimators, diagnostics
  - `run.R`: four studies, writes `results.rds` (seed 20260521)
  - `figures.R`: figure generation

## The four theorems

- T1 glass ceiling (`identifiability.tex`): (prevalence, sensitivity,
  specificity) non-identifiable from code data alone.
- T2 identifiability with chart review (`identifiability.tex`): a
  chart-reviewed subsample identifies the coding model and hence
  prevalence. Analog of the ERCC spike-in theorem in scrna-coarsening.
- T3 code-frequency consistency (`identifiability.tex`): a fitted model
  reproduces marginal code frequencies exactly; marginal-fit checks
  cannot detect prevalence bias. Analog of cell-total consistency.
- T4 bias bound under informative coding (`methodology.tex`): code-only
  bias bounded by the severity-coding correlation; calibrated-estimator
  residual bias bounded by the case-mix gap. The case-mix gap is the
  analog of the ERCC-endogenous capture-efficiency gap.

## Conventions (Alex's preferences)

- **No em-dashes** (soul plugin hook enforces; use commas, colons,
  periods, parentheses).
- **No vanity counts** as achievement filler (describe the work, not
  page/reference/theorem counts; normal enumeration of T1 to T4 is fine).

- Tight conference-style writing: prefer one-paragraph subsections.
- Cite `towell2026scrnacoarsening` / `towell2026spatialcoarsening` /
  `towell2026masked` for shared theorem proofs rather than re-deriving.
- Author: Alexander Towell, lex@metafunctor.com, SIUE Department of
  Computer Science.

## Prior-art discipline

Do NOT claim to invent latent-class phenotyping or sensitivity /
specificity estimation without a gold standard. Hui & Walter (1980) and
Dawid & Skene (1979) are the ancestors and must be cited as such in
`discussion.tex`. The real contribution is the masked-cause
unification, the C1/C2/C3 classification, the explicit glass-ceiling
construction, and the informative-coding bias bound.
